Commute question
Your survey asks about commute time using three broad ranges. Please ask for the actual amount of time instead, in minutes or hours. Commute time is not a sensitive question, so respondents do not need the protection of a range, and exact values give you more information for your analysis.

Energy exerted at work
Your survey asks students to rate how much energy they exert at work, but it never defines what that means. Respondents could think of mental effort, physical effort, or both, and this is a subjective question, so different people will answer it differently. Please add a short definition of energy exertion. It would also help to describe what a 1 and a 10 on your scale represent, so everyone rates on a similar basis.
