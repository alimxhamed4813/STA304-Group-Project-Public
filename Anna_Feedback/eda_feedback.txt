Figure 2 jitter

The points in Figure 2 are jittered, but neither the plot nor the caption says so. Your code only sets a width, with no height, so the jitter is horizontal and the values themselves are not changed. That is fine for a boxplot. Without a note, though, it looks odd that some points are spread further apart horizontally than others when the categories are only No and Yes. Add a line to the caption saying that horizontal jitter was added to reduce overlap.

Figure 5 notation

In Figure 5 you write the correlation coefficient as R. It should be the Greek letter rho, written in math mode as $\rho$.
